The Story

What this experience was like

Joining BarkStudio allowed me to apply my passion for graphic design beyond the classroom. As the Associate Director for Visual and Creatives, I was responsible for creating publication materials that supported the organization's coverage of university events, announcements, and UAAP competitions.

Working in a student media organization also meant adapting to tight deadlines and constantly changing priorities. Event schedules could shift with little notice, requiring quick revisions and efficient collaboration with editors and content creators. Over time, I became more confident in managing multiple design tasks, accepting feedback, and producing quality work under pressure. The experience taught me that great creative work is not only about aesthetics but also about teamwork, communication, and reliability.
