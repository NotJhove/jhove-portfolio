The Story

What this experience was like

As a Software QA Intern, I worked with web applications, AI-powered plugins, and e-commerce platforms such as Magento, Shopify, and WordPress. My responsibilities included creating and maintaining automated test scripts using Playwright and BugBug, executing test cases, reporting defects, and verifying fixes. One of the highlights of my internship was helping migrate automated tests from BugBug to Playwright, making the test suite more flexible and maintainable.

The internship challenged me to think beyond simply finding bugs. I learned how to analyze application behavior, write reliable automation scripts, and work closely with developers to resolve issues. With each project, I became more confident in debugging, testing, and adapting to new tools and technologies.
