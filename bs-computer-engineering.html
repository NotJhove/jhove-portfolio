The Story

What this experience was like

My journey in Computer Engineering began with learning the fundamentals of programming, electronics, and digital logic before gradually progressing into more advanced topics such as embedded systems, computer architecture, software engineering, networking, and data science. Through laboratory exercises, coursework, and team projects, I gained experience turning theoretical concepts into working solutions. One of the memorable milestones was developing our capstone project, Guardian Vision, an AI-powered surveillance system that combined computer vision, machine learning, and software development to support post-incident investigations.

Throughout the program, I learned that engineering is just as much about persistence as it is about technical knowledge. Many projects required debugging hardware connections, troubleshooting software issues, and collaborating with teammates under tight deadlines. Those experiences taught me to approach problems methodically, communicate effectively within a team, and continuously improve my work through testing, feedback, and iteration. By the time I graduated, I had developed not only stronger technical skills but also the confidence to take on unfamiliar challenges and keep learning beyond the classroom.
