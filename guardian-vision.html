Overview

What is Guardian Vision?

Guardian Vision is an AI-powered multi-camera surveillance system developed to assist security personnel with post-incident investigations in small establishments. By combining computer vision and action recognition technologies, the system automatically detects people, tracks their movement across camera views, recognizes specific human gestures, and timestamps important events for faster evidence retrieval.

Instead of manually reviewing hours of surveillance footage, users can quickly search for detected actions, review categorized incidents, and save relevant video clips for further investigation. Guardian Vision streamlines the investigation process while maintaining data privacy through secure local storage and automatic deletion of stored footage after 30 days.
